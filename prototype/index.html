How it works

Eight taps, then a number you can explain

The calculator runs the Supreme Court’s method (Sarla Verma, Pranay Sethi and the cases since) on the facts you give. The estimate appears from the third answer and sharpens as you go.

1Say what happenedWho was hurt or lost, how they were involved, whether the vehicle was found. Three checks tell you if a tribunal claim exists at all.
2Watch the estimate narrowAge, work, family and the medical certificate each move a live band. Nothing is a promise: the court decides the final amount.
3Check any offer, collect the papersPut the insurer’s figure beside yours with interest priced in, then take the list of papers to free legal aid.
